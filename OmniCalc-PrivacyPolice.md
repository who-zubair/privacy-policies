By downloading, accessing, or using OmniCalc, you signify your acceptance of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree to this policy, please do not use our Application.

## 1. Summary & Core Philosophy

OmniCalc is built primarily as an offline-first, high-precision utility application. 
- **Calculations & History:** All mathematical equations, calculations, conversion values, and custom configurations remain strictly on your local device. We do not transmit or upload your calculations to any external servers.
- **Account Registration:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide personal credentials to use OmniCalc.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
OmniCalc saves certain operational preferences solely to your device's local storage (via secure on-device sandboxed storage):
- **User Interface Preferences:** Selected skin/theme, sound effects toggles, haptic feedback settings, number formatting options, and secret unlocked state.
- **Local Calculation History:** Past calculations stored locally on your device for quick reference.
- *None of this data is transmitted, synchronized, or sold to remote databases or third parties.*

### B. Automatically Collected Technical Data & Diagnostics
When using mobile applications, certain standard technical data may be automatically generated by your device's operating system or through standard Google Play Services for stability and crash analysis:
- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, system language, screen resolution, and regional hardware specifications.
- **Application Performance & Crash Data:** Error stack traces, application state logs at the time of an unexpected crash, and aggregate performance metrics. This information is purely used to diagnose bugs and improve app reliability.

### C. Future Third-Party Advertising and Analytics Data
To support the ongoing maintenance and development of OmniCalc, we may integrate reputable third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob) in future updates. These third parties may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable information according to their respective privacy policies, including:
- **Advertising Identifiers:** Android Advertising ID (AAID/GAID).
- **Ad Interaction Data:** Views, clicks, impressions, and engagement metrics.
- **Approximate Location:** General network-derived location (country/city level) used solely for regional ad relevance and fraud prevention.
- **Cookies and Tracking Technologies:** Mobile tracking tokens used by ad providers to measure ad performance, prevent repetitive ad impressions, and combat ad fraud.

## 5. User Control, Opt-Outs & Data Management

You have complete control over your data within OmniCalc:

- **Clearing Stored App Data:** You can instantly delete all local calculation histories, saved settings, and skin preferences at any time by navigating to your Android device settings:  
  `Settings > Apps > OmniCalc > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage`.
- **Resetting Advertising ID:** You can reset or delete your device's Google Advertising ID at any time via:  
  `Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID` or `Delete advertising ID`.
- **Personalized Ads Opt-Out:** You may opt out of interest-based ads by enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or withdrawing ad consent through on-screen consent prompts (if applicable).
- **Uninstalling the App:** Deleting or uninstalling OmniCalc completely removes all local sandboxed application data from your device.
